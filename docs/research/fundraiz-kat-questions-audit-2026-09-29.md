# Fundraiz: Kat's questions checked against public sources

Research date: 29 September 2026. Official pages were freshly read in the browser; no account was created or vendor contacted. This supplements the [CRM fit research](fundraiz-crm-fit-2026-09-27.md) and [meeting-question audit](fundraiz-meeting-question-audit-2026-09-28.md). Published vendor statements establish what is advertised, not successful operation in DFN's configuration.

## Support: facts to use as the starting point

The [contact page](https://www.fundraiz.ai/contact) publishes:

- Email: **info@fundraiz.ai**, with a typical response within **24 hours**.
- Phone: **+61 4 0231 9692**, **Monday–Friday, 8am–6pm AEST**, using the site's exact timezone label.
- Ongoing support via **email, chat and phone**; the contact form includes Technical Support.

[Pricing](https://www.fundraiz.ai/pricing) says all plans include 24/7 support, while Starter names email support, Growth priority support, and Enterprise a dedicated account manager and SLA. These statements do not establish 24/7 live phone support, a severity-based incident response, or a guaranteed resolution time. Ask about the proposed service commitment rather than repeat the generic contact question. The Trust Centre's security-enquiry targets are a separate category, not a product-support SLA.

## Roadmap and delivery: distinguish released from promised

Fundraiz's [24 August 2026 update](https://www.fundraiz.ai/blog/we-are-atc-2026-finalists-here-is-what-that-means-to-us) says its first-year updates included **Prospect Research, Giving Impact Statements, Grants Management and Prompt to Campaign**. These are vendor statements about shipped features, not dates for future delivery. No public feature-priority roadmap or customer voting mechanism was found in the reviewed blog, contact and product pages.

The [terms](https://www.fundraiz.ai/terms) say customer ideas can be used without obligation and reserve service-change rights. Consequently, informal willingness to discuss a feature should not be treated as a contractual delivery promise. A roadmap discussion is useful for future fit; DFN's required current workflows still need to work without depending on uncommitted development.

## Migration and post-live claims

The [Coastal Community Foundation story](https://www.fundraiz.ai/case-studies/coastal-community-foundation) describes a two-week onboarding in which Fundraiz's team helped deduplicate records and merge giving history from three systems. This is more concrete than generic migration assistance but is vendor-authored, describes another data model and does not establish DFN's timeline. The [contact FAQ](https://www.fundraiz.ai/contact) gives a general 24–48-hour start claim; neither figure should replace an estimate based on DFN's linked sponsorship, finance and communications history.

## Cost precision

The [terms](https://www.fundraiz.ai/terms) state fees exclude applicable taxes, default to AUD unless otherwise specified, and increases receive at least 30 days' notice. The [homepage](https://www.fundraiz.ai/) explicitly presents its plan prices in USD. A written quote should settle currency, tax, inclusions and limits. No reviewed source establishes that SMS delivery, third-party processor fees or bespoke integration maintenance are all covered by the headline subscription.

## Recommendation for the meeting

Kat's Microsoft-workflow, post-live-struggles and roadmap questions add useful dimensions. Keep migration ownership, urgent support and complete cost, but begin from the facts already published. Replace broad API/integration capability questions with the boundary between Fundraiz-owned workflows and DFN's retained website, Stripe and event tools. None of these findings justifies promising a Microsoft-suite replacement or free unlimited SMS.
