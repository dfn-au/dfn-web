# How separate should the AU and NZ sites feel?

**Answered on 15 October:** local contact details, separate donations/events and an obvious way to switch sites. See [the outcome](https://github.com/dfn-au/dfn-web/issues/68).

Original prompt: The footer is our first example. When someone visits the AU site, should it feel like:

- **One organisation, viewing Australia:** show address, phone and email for both offices?
- **The Australian site:** show only Australian contact details, with the equivalent approach on NZ?

Should this approach apply throughout the site, or are there exceptions?

# How obvious should the country be?

**Partly answered on 15 October:** country switching must be clear; the similar flags are a concern. The final visual treatment is still for design review.

Original prompt: How easily should visitors recognise whether they are on the AU or NZ site?

- **Prominent:** a flag or clearly visible country label?
- **Subtle:** the browser address (dfn.org.au or dfn.org.nz) plus a small logo difference, such as “Dignity Freedom Network AU”?

Does it need to be clear at a glance on every page, including on mobile?
